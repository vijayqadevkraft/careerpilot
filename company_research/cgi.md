## 2. Culture & Core Identity: The "Ownership" Model

- **"Together, as owners":** CGI's defining cultural differentiator. Employees are referred to as **"Members"** and a large proportion participate in the Share Purchase Plan, giving them an owner's stake in client satisfaction and company performance.
- **Client Proximity Model:** CGI pairs local relationship teams who understand local business dynamics with global delivery centers (including major tech delivery hubs in India: Bengaluru, Chennai, Hyderabad, Mumbai).
- **Core Pillars:**
  - Client satisfaction & operational excellence
  - Member well-being & continuous learning
  - Inclusion, Diversity, and Community engagement

As a Senior QA Lead, I approach test architecture with that exact mindset—owning the product quality, treating client release stability as personal accountability, and collaborating transparently across engineering teams."*

Having built automated testing frameworks for high-stakes enterprise platforms like HDFC Life and TVS Motor, I thrive in environments where software reliability is paramount."*
